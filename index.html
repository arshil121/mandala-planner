About Mandala Planner

Mandala Planner is a free online tool for goal setting with a mandala chart, also known as Mandal-Art or a 9×9 goal matrix. Write one main goal in the center, surround it with eight strategic areas, then turn each area into eight concrete actions. That gives you 64 actions you can plan, schedule and track.

Pick five to eight priorities for the week, set dates and times, and export them as a calendar file you can open in Google Calendar, Outlook or Apple Calendar. You can keep a separate mandala for each goal or project.

Frequently asked questions

What is a mandala chart?

A mandala chart is a 3×3 grid with a goal in the center and eight supporting areas around it. Each area becomes the center of its own 3×3 grid, which produces a 9×9 chart with 64 actions.

Is the online Mandala Planner free?

Yes. It is free to use and needs no account or sign-up.

Where is my data stored?

Only in your own browser (localStorage), on your device. Nothing is uploaded to a server. It does not sync between devices, so download a JSON backup from the Data tab now and then.

Can I add my actions to a calendar?

Yes. Give actions a date and optional start time, then export them as an .ics file and import it into your calendar app.

Can I make more than one mandala?

Yes. Use New mandala at the top to create a separate chart for each goal or project.
